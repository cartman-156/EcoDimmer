EcoDimmer allows you to dim your screen further than your system settings allow. Perfect for night-time reading or battery saving on AMOLED screens.

Unlike most other dimming apps, EcoDimmer uses an Accessibility Service overlay to ensure that the status bar, notification shade, and navigation bar are also dimmed for a consistent experience.

Key Features:
- Adjustable Dimming Intensity (10% to 90%)
- Shake to Rescue: Vigorous shake gesture to emergency-disable the dimmer.
- Scheduled Dimming: Set automatic ON/OFF times.
- Privacy First: Requires zero standard permissions. No internet access. No screen reading.
- Stealthy: Hides from app drawer. Access settings via QS Tile long-press.

Built with performance and privacy in mind. Created by Antigravity.
